### Narrative Flow (for internal use)

1. **Awareness** – A sudden API outage or a security alert pushes the target (CTO, Platform Engineer, or Head of Security) to search for solutions. Guardrail captures attention with a free health‑score scan and a concise playbook, turning panic into a concrete next step.

2. **Consideration** – The health‑score reveals a high‑risk exposure. The prospect evaluates guardrail against building an in‑house fix or using native cloud tools. An ROI calculator and transparent pricing reduce uncertainty and highlight the multi‑cloud advantage.

3. **Trial** – A frictionless 14‑day trial with a guided wizard and live assistance lets the team protect an endpoint in minutes. Adaptive alert tuning prevents noise, keeping the experience positive.

4. **Adoption** – Quantifiable results (downtime avoided, abuse reduced) are presented in a clear dashboard, enabling finance to approve a subscription. A risk‑free first paid month and ready‑made integrations seal the deal.

5. **Expansion** – As the company adds more APIs and regulatory requirements, guardrail’s flexible policy engine, volume discounts, and community program encourage upsell and cross‑sell, driving a healthy expansion rate.
